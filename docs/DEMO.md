# Walkthrough and video plan

Use real Foundry mode to demonstrate AI generation. In offline mode, describe the labelled templates as templates. Prepare the selected preferences before recording; distinguish cached responses from newly generated responses.

## Walkthrough

1. Play the synthetic match. Observe that facts update independently of model generation. Optionally enable Pause for AI at key moments in Key moments.
2. After the goal at 60:30 has occurred, pause and select it from Key moments. Inspect the recovery-to-shot phase, its sequence and supporting events.
3. Compare Fan/Analyst or a team perspective. Allow the request to finish, then inspect its source label and evidence.
4. Generate a recap through the current playhead.
5. Open Broadcast desk below On the pitch / The Big Picture. Inspect the broadcast preview and shared JSON export.
6. Withdraw the demo goal. Observe the revised score, affected-content withdrawals and replacement output. Export the correction patch.
7. Reset the correction to return to the original record.

The separate developer validation test injects artificial incorrect claims. It is a test fixture and is distinct from correcting the synthetic match record.

## Suggested video, under two minutes

- 0–15 seconds: introduce the problem and the running match.
- 15–45 seconds: explain the 60:30 sequence and show evidence.
- 45–65 seconds: compare a preflighted audience/team perspective.
- 65–85 seconds: show a recap and broadcast preview.
- 85–110 seconds: correct the goal record and show withdrawals, replacement and correction export.

The sequence may be prepared at the relevant playhead before recording. Keep source labels visible. Use fictional assets and permitted material.
